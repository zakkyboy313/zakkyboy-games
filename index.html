Games for the ZakkyBoy handheld. Pick one and install it straight from your browser.

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Plug in your ZakkyBoy with a USB-C data cable.
  3. Click Install, pick the port that appears, then Connect.
  4. Wait for Installation complete, then press reset.

No port in the list?

Try another cable (some only charge). Or hold BOOT on the board while plugging in, then click Install again.

Buttons don't respond?

Your buttons are on different pins from the ones listed on the card. Ask Zak for a version built for your wiring.

One game at a time

Installing a game replaces the one on the console. Zakky Pixel + Zakky Gun puts two on at once.
